THE GUARDIANS

United across the Sky, the Core, the Sea, and the Peaks.

From the molten depths to the frozen peaks — the glacier wolf who refused to stop running, the firebird who never looked away, and every warrior who answered the call.

Glacier Wolf · Frost / Spirit

Ice Heart

The resistance's driving force. A resolute glacier wolf who crossed every biome on a single mission: rescue his brother from the Central AI's processing pods. Static-Frost gauntlets, frost-shattering blows, and a will that convinced a Sky Queen and cracked the AI's core. He fights not as a soldier — but as a brother who refuses to stop running.

Firebird · Volcanic / Avian

Hotly

The one who didn't look away. While others returned to their games, Hotly tracked the alien exhaust trails north alone. Her fire rises with her fury. She was first to see the invaders as colonizers, and she saved Ice Heart from the Mirror of Frost with a thermal surge that nearly burned her out. Fierce, aerial, and carrying the resistance's anger so others can carry hope.
